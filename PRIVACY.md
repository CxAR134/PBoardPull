# Privacy Policy: Board Pack (PBoardPull)

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Board Pack is a personal tool made by Curated By AR for its owner's own use. It turns one of the owner's own Pinterest boards into a single PDF.

## What it accesses

Using the Pinterest API and an access token the owner generated for their own account, the app reads the owner's own boards and pins: titles, descriptions, link URLs, and image URLs. It does not create, edit, or delete anything on Pinterest.

## How data is handled

- Pin images are fetched through the app's server only so they can be drawn into a PDF on the owner's device.
- The app has no database and does not save pin data. Nothing is kept after a request finishes.
- The PDF is built on the owner's device. It leaves the device only when the owner chooses to share it.

## What is stored on the device

The browser's local storage holds an access key (so the owner doesn't have to retype it) and a sheet-size preference. The app uses no cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

## Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. The app talks to Pinterest, to read the owner's boards, and to its hosting provider, Vercel, which runs the app and may keep standard request logs.
